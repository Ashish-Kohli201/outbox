# Code of Conduct

We want Outbox to be a friendly place to build something useful, especially for people early in their careers.

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unwelcome advances, in any form.
- Don't share other people's private information.
- Don't use the project to build tools for spam, scraping or deceiving people.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly do.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through GitHub (a private message or the repository's Security tab for
sensitive reports). All reports are treated confidentially.

This is a short version of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which applies in full as the reference for anything not covered here.
